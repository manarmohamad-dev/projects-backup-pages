PRIVATE BACKUP UTILITY

Backups kept private.
Recovery kept practical.

Projects Backup is a personal utility for storing encrypted backups of applications managed by its owner. It is not a public file-sharing or backup service.

What the utility does

Its intended workflow creates database and application-file backups, encrypts them before upload, and stores them in the owner's Google Drive. A separate local copy and isolated recovery tests support restoration.

The workflow is being configured. This page does not claim that scheduled backups or production recovery have already been verified.

Why Google access is needed

The owner authorizes Google Drive access to upload encrypted backup files, inspect backup metadata, and download files for recovery checks. This authorization is separate from the applications' Google Sign-In integrations.

What is public

Only this description and its policies are published here. This website has no login form, upload form, backup download links, tracking scripts, or credentials.
