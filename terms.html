Personal use

Projects Backup is an owner-operated utility, not a commercial backup service or an invitation for public account registration. Only data the operator is authorized to back up may be processed.

Operational responsibility

The operator is responsible for access control, protecting encryption keys, reviewing retention, monitoring backup failures, and testing restoration. Source databases must not be overwritten during recovery testing. Availability of Google Drive and GitHub Pages is subject to those providers' terms.

No recovery guarantee

The workflow is being configured and is provided without a service-level commitment. A completed upload alone does not establish that a backup is recoverable. Independent copies and successful recovery tests are required before relying on it.
