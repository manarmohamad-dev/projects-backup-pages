Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Purpose and scope

Projects Backup is a personal backup utility operated by the owner of the manarmohamad-dev account. This policy covers its Google Drive integration and this informational website. It is not a service offered to the public.

Information handled

When configured and authorized, the utility handles encrypted backup archives and their metadata, including names, sizes, timestamps and integrity checksums. Archives may contain application records, database contents and files needed for recovery. Sensitive configuration, if included for recovery, must also be encrypted.

Google OAuth credentials and access or refresh tokens are used to authenticate authorized operations. They are stored separately from this public website and repository and must not be included in logs or published files. Drive permissions requested by the utility are shown on Google's consent screen.

Use of Google data

Google Drive access is used only for the owner's backup storage and recovery workflow: uploading archives, listing and verifying backup files, downloading them for recovery, and managing retention when explicitly configured. The utility does not use Gmail or Google Photos APIs and does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, sale, or training AI models.

Storage, access and sharing

Backups are encrypted before upload. The planned destinations are a private folder in the owner's Google Drive and a local copy under the owner's control. Temporary encrypted staging files may be held on the server. Decryption keys are kept separately from server upload credentials.

Backup contents are not intentionally shared with other users. Google provides cloud storage and GitHub hosts this public informational site. The utility does not sell personal data. Encryption does not hide all metadata from storage providers, and no system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the policy configured by the owner and available storage. Automatic retention is not yet enabled. Deleting backup copies is separate from changing source application records; the utility must not delete or reset original databases as part of routine backup operations.

The owner can remove backup copies from each destination and revoke the application's access in Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase existing backups. Provider trash and retention rules may apply.

This website

This site has no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or application cookies. GitHub Pages may process technical request information such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement. Visiting this site does not authorize Google Drive access.

Contact and changes

General questions may be submitted through the project issue tracker. Do not include secrets or personal records. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
